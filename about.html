Our Story & Philosophy

“A home away from home, but with better coffee!” Learn how a historic home in Pasadena became the community hub for conversation, local art, and handcrafted coffee.

Welcome to the Best Coffee Shop in the Houston Area

Right outside the city in Pasadena, TX, with a true love for fine coffee and wanting to establish a place that is quaint and off the beaten path, the Golden Acres area of Pasadena seemed to be the perfect location. Located across the street from Silver Sycamore, Sycamore Grounds fits right in. We created an area for guests to get away from the bustling city and be taken back to a simpler time.

“Our main focus is providing a true 'Local Coffee Shop' where guests can come to enjoy the finest quality coffee, fresh, in-house made pastries, and our famous breakfast tacos made daily.”

Roasting with Amaya Roasting Co.

We serve and sell coffee roasted in a manner that accentuates its distinctions, balancing acidity, sweetness, and depth of flavors. The best part is that we keep it strictly local with our coffee, roasted right here by Houston Coffee Roasters, Amaya Roasting Co.

Supporting Local is Our Standard

Supporting local businesses is not a trend for us—it's who we are. We serve locally roasted coffee, sweeten our drinks with 100% pure local Texas raw honey, display original paintings and pieces by local Texas artists on our walls, and even offer cold-pressed juices from local purveyors.

An Artistically Inspired Atmosphere

Served in a warm, rustic, old-style artistically inspired atmosphere, Sycamore Grounds allows you to sit back, listen to warm acoustic music, and take in the collections of local art. It's the perfect sanctuary for studying, reading, entrepreneurs conducting business meetings, playing a friendly game of chess with locals on the porch, or simply finding quiet time with your favorite drink.

Now is the Perfect Time!

Come meet our baristas, have a delicious coffee drink, and relax—because making you feel right at home is what we are all about!
