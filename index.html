Our Heritage

A Home Away From Home, With Better Coffee.

“To provide our customers with top quality coffee, but more than that, top quality atmosphere. A place for you to come relax, grab a cup of coffee and one of our homemade pastries, and hang out.”

Established in 2012 in the historic Golden Acres area of Pasadena, TX (located across from Silver Sycamore), Sycamore Grounds was born out of a genuine passion for fine coffee and community connection. We lovingly restored historic houses to create an oasis where guests escape city rush and step into a simpler, warmer time.

Supporting local is our everyday standard. We proudly partner with Houston's Amaya Roasting Co. to serve beans roasted to accentuate natural acidity and rich sweetness. We use raw local honey, local cold-pressed juices, display pieces by local artists on our walls, and invite you to play chess on our porches.
